mmcif-tools

Small, single-purpose command-line tools that select, delete, edit, convert and inspect mmCIF files, and chain together with Unix pipes. They need only the Python standard library (Python 3.9 or newer).

mmcif_fetch 1brs | mmcif_selchain -A,D | mmcif_delentity -non-polymer,water | mmcif_reres -1 | mmcif_delmeta > clean.cif

Run in your browser

Open mmCIF files and chain the tools into a pipeline, with nothing to install. The files are read on your computer and never uploaded.
